# Contributing

Thanks for stopping by.

**Issues are welcome.** If one of the free skills (`wcag-contrast-check`, `habit-tracker-pdf`) gives a
wrong result, crashes or prints badly, please open an issue with:

- the exact command you ran (or what you asked your agent),
- the output or error message,
- your Python version and operating system,
- for PDFs: the viewer or printer you used, and a screenshot if you can.

Ideas for small improvements are welcome too.

**Pull requests:** small, focused fixes to the free skills are considered. Please keep them to the
Python standard library and run the script on a few inputs before submitting. By contributing you
agree that your contribution is licensed under the MIT licence of those skills.

**Not accepted here:** changes to the showcase images or branding, and requests for the paid
prompts or paid skills. Those are sold only on [PromptBase](https://promptbase.com/profile/bentleyartdesign?via=bentleyartdesign)
and [Agensi](https://www.agensi.io/creators/duncan-bentley); for questions about a purchase, please
contact me through the marketplace you bought from.
